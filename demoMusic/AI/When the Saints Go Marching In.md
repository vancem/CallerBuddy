# When the Saints go Marching In

_Traditional_\

## Opener

Four Ladies Chain, across that old ring\
You Rollaway and Circle Left\
Four Ladies Rollaway and Circle Left\
Left Allemande and Weave the Ring\
Oh, when the Saints go marching in\
Oh, when the Saints go marching in (Swing and Promenade)\
Oh how I want to be in that number\
When the Saints go marching in\

## Figure

Heads Promenade go half way round\
Come in and square thru go four\
Go all the way to a right and left thru\
Veer Left and Ferris Wheel. (Centers)\
Square thru go three hands round\
Swing your corner and Promenade\
Oh how I want to be in that number\
When the Saints go marching in\
